§ 01 — The House

Crafting California's finest gassy cannabis since before it was legal.

Kush Company earned its place as one of California's most respected cannabis houses through meticulous breeding, bold flavors, and a refusal to chase trends. At the heart of the work is a deep understanding of genetics and a reverence for legacy OG profiles.

The Super Unleaded line — born from a hand-selected cross of KushCo OG and Sour Power OG — is our flagship. We took a standout male from the original cross and pollinated our entire catalog with it. What came back was a family of hybrids that all carry the same fuel DNA, each with its own personality: creamy, sour, citrus, sherbet, funk.

Every batch is grown to express its full potential. Dense, frost-covered, richly aromatic. Buds that live up to the expectations of seasoned smokers who demand only the best.
