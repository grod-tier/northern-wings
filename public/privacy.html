Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2025

Who we are

Northern Wings is a youth-led conservation enterprise operating Monarch Waystation #54310. We sell monarch habitat kits and share educational resources about native pollinators. Our website is northernwings.co.

What information we collect

When you purchase a kit, we collect:

  • Your name, email address, and shipping address
  • Payment information (processed securely by Stripe — we never store card details)
  • Order history and transaction records

When you visit our website, we may automatically receive standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising networks.

How we use your information

We use your information solely to:

  • Fulfill and ship your order
  • Send order confirmations and shipping updates
  • Respond to customer service inquiries
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Payment processing

All payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. We do not store credit card numbers or payment card data on our servers. Stripe's privacy policy governs the handling of your payment information and is available at stripe.com/privacy.

Data retention

We retain order and transaction records for a minimum of 7 years to comply with tax and financial record-keeping requirements. You may request deletion of your personal information by emailing us; where we are required by law to retain records, we will honor that obligation while deleting other data where possible.

Your rights

You may request to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to legal retention requirements)

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@northernwings.co.

Cookies

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. We use only functional session data necessary for checkout (via Stripe's secure payment flow).

Children's privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

Questions? Reach us at hello@northernwings.co.
