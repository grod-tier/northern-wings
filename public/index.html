Our Mission

Safe havens for monarchs, one milkweed at a time.

Northern Wings is a youth-led social enterprise that manages over an acre of land specifically for Monarch butterfly conservation. Known as a Monarch Waystation, this land provides critical habitat for migrating monarchs — offering milkweed, nectar plants, and a safe place to rest and reproduce.

To support this effort, we sell DIY Pollinator Garden Kits so others can establish their own waystations or enhance their existing gardens. Together, we can help reverse the decline of one of North America's most iconic species.

Certified Monarch Waystation #54310

A real waystation on a real migration corridor.

Northern Wings is a certified Monarch Waystation located in Emmet County, MI — at the northernmost tip of Michigan's Lower Peninsula, where monarchs funnel through each fall on their journey to Mexico.

The waystation is larger than average, built on private farmland managed specifically for wildlife. Every kit sold directly supports the restoration of more habitat along this critical migratory route.

Meet the Founder

Conservation is personal.

I am a high school student passionate about the environment. As someone who spends a great deal of time outdoors — hunting, fishing, hiking, skiing, and photographing wildlife — nature conservation is a cause that's very close to my heart.

Recently, I've been researching the decline of the Monarch butterfly population, particularly the impact of habitat loss, and I'm eager to contribute meaningfully to conservation efforts.

Merging my interests in both conservation and community engagement, I started the Northern Waystation Project to help protect monarch butterflies and raise awareness about the importance of native plants and pollinators.
