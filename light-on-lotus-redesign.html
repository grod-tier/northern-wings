About Kate & the studio

A sacred, uplifting space in the heart of the Westside.

Light on Lotus was founded by Kate Duyn in 2016 with one intention: create a place where all can come for support, community, and inspiration — a place to Om, move, and groove together.

The studio offers classes for every level, including prenatal yoga, with creative sequencing, inside or out in our garden (weather permitting), and live-streamed for practice from home. Teacher trainings, workshops, kirtans, soundbaths, and a Wellness Wing with massage, acupuncture, and reiki round out the experience.
